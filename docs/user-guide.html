Part 1

Manufacturer team, in ERPNext

How data arrives

Distributor's TallyPrime→SSV agent on the same PC keeps only your items→Signed upload→Your ERPNext: SSV Channel

The agent reads the distributor's bills and closing stock from TallyPrime every few minutes (you set how often). It sends only the lines for items under your stock group, so other brands on the same bill never leave the distributor's PC. Edits and deletions in Tally follow automatically. Nothing here posts to your accounts or your own stock ledger.

A distributor that sells for several manufacturers runs one agent for all of them. It sends through the SSV hub, which delivers each manufacturer's lines to that manufacturer's ERPNext only. Your reports look the same either way.

Log in

Open your site, for the pilot ssv-test.rajlaxmiworld.in, and sign in. Everything lives in the SSV Channel workspace in the sidebar; the SSV Dashboard shortcut is at its top.

Give each colleague their own login (User → New) with the role Sales Manager to read reports, or System Manager to manage distributors. Keep Administrator for emergencies.

Daily two-minute check

  1. Open SSV Sync Health. Every distributor should be OK. The Reconciled column shows when your site last checked its totals against the agent's (agent 0.5.3+).

    StatusMeaningDo this
    OKA successful run recentlyNothing
    FailingThe agent runs but its last run failed; the Problem column says why, e.g. TallyPrime not openCall the distributor with the problem text
    StaleNo successful run or data for 24 hours (or twice the distributor's interval, if longer)Ask whether the PC and TallyPrime are on
    SilentNothing for three times that long, or neverCheck the agent is installed; see Troubleshooting
    ErrorsBatches refused in the periodOpen SSV Ingest Log, filter Status = Rejected, read the reason
    MismatchOnce a day the agent sends totals of what it delivered: bills, quantity and value per month. They differ from what your site holds. The Problem column names the months.Tell your SSV support contact, and give them the Problem text

  2. Glance at the SSV Dashboard: quarter, zone, month-on-month against last year, top categories and brands. The line under its title reads Data up to date from 3 of 3 distributors. When it turns orange, it names the distributors behind: until they catch up, the figures are short by their share.

  3. If a distributor's figures look old, open it (SSV Distributor) and press Sync Now. A running agent starts within about 2 minutes, and the line at the top of the form shows the result.

Using a report

  • Period: pick a preset (current FY, quarter, month, week, last week, last month, last quarter, last 15 days) or set From and To. Weeks run Sunday to Saturday.
  • Tree: rows open level by level; the arrows expand and collapse. Every level carries its own sub-total.
  • Chart: each main report draws a chart above the table from the same figures, largest first; month columns draw as lines.
  • Filters: distributor, territory (a zone or state includes everything under it) and item work in every report.
  • Export and print: the ⋯ menu at the top right exports to Excel or prints.

What the figures mean

Net sale
Sales out minus credit notes (returns) in. Deleted and cancelled bills are left out.
Purchase
What the distributor bought, minus purchase returns.
Other
The balancing figure in Stock Summary: godown transfers, delivery notes not yet invoiced, and any difference between Tally's counted stock and the bills. Opening + Purchase − Net sale + Other = Closing on every row.
Closing stock
Tally's own figure, sent daily and after every change. Negative stock is shown in red, as Tally allows it.
Unallocated
In Godown-wise stock: the part of Tally's item total the godowns do not add up to.
Outlets billed
Distinct retailers billed in the period, counted at every level (never summed).
(unmapped)
A Tally item not yet linked to one of your Items. See Items and mapping.
Deleted in Tally
A bill deleted in the distributor's Tally. It stays visible on the bill, marked, and drops out of every report.

Adding a distributor

  1. SSV Distributor → New. Distributor ID (e.g. DIST-002), name, and Territory set to the city: its parents give the state and zone in reports.

  2. Under Which Tally items belong to this principal, add a rule with your stock group spelled exactly as in that distributor's Tally, for example Stock Group = Ceat Tyre. Sub-groups are included.

  3. Set Sync Every (Minutes): 5 to 1440 (once a day). Save.

  4. Actions → Show Agent Secret shows five lines. Send them to whoever sets up the distributor's PC, privately; they are the key to that distributor's account.

  5. Record the distributor's Data Sharing Consent Date. Tick Shares Cost Price only if they agreed; margin columns also need SSV Settings → Show Cost And Margin.

    To make that a hard rule, tick SSV Settings → Hold Data Until Consent Is Recorded. Your site then accepts no data from a distributor until its consent date is filled in. Its agent keeps the data and retries, so nothing is lost.

Items and mapping

Reports group by the Channel Classification fields on each Item: Product Line, Channel Category, Sub-Category, Pack Size, Size Group, Size Sort Order and Product Class, plus Brand. Empty fields show as "(not set)".

A Tally item is linked automatically when its name or code equals one of your Items. Others appear in SSV Channel Item as Unmapped: open one, set Item, save. Past bills follow the new link at once.

Distributors name items their own way, so the site suggests the closest of your Items. It compares words, sizes and part numbers, and never suggests a different size. In the list, those items show as Suggested.

  • To accept one, open it, check the suggestion and press Use Suggestion.
  • To accept many, tick them in the list and choose Actions → Accept Suggested Items.
  • After adding new Items, press Refresh Suggestions.

Nothing is linked without someone accepting it.

Cases and pieces. If a distributor keeps an item in another unit, set Conversion Factor on its SSV Channel Item: how many of your units make one of theirs, for example 12 if they count cases of 12 and you count pieces. Quantities in every report, past ones included, change at once; values stay as billed.

Alerts

Put addresses in SSV Settings → Send Alerts To. Each morning one e-mail lists:

  • failing distributors, with the reason;
  • silent distributors;
  • distributors whose totals differ from the agent's;
  • items whose latest closing stock is negative.

No e-mail is sent when all is well. The site needs an outgoing e-mail account. To check the setup, press Send Test Alert on SSV Settings: it sends today's alert at once, or says what's missing.

When a distributor leaves

When a distributor's consent ends, the consent letter promises to delete its data.

  1. Open the SSV Distributor, untick Enabled and save. No new data is accepted from then on.

  2. Actions → Delete Channel Data, and type the Distributor ID to confirm. Every bill, stock figure, item, retailer and log received from that distributor is deleted. The old agent's secret is replaced, so it can't send again.

The distributor record itself stays, with its consent date and a note of who deleted what and when.

Part 2

Distributor PC, the agent

What is needed

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC that runs the distributor's TallyPrime, with internet access.
  • TallyPrime open with the distributor's company. In TallyPrime: F1 Help → Settings → Connectivity, set TallyPrime acts as to Both or Server, port 9000.
  • The agent package (ssv-agent-…-win-x64.zip) and the five settings lines from the manufacturer.
  • An administrator login on the PC, once, for the install.

Install

  1. Unzip the package into any folder, then double-click install.cmd and accept the Windows prompt.

    It installs the SSV Tally Connector service, which starts with Windows and restarts itself if it stops. It also shows a setup password (write it down), puts an SSV Agent Setup shortcut on the desktop, and opens the setup page.

  2. On the setup page, sign in with that password.

    • Connection: paste the lines from the manufacturer's Show Agent Secret, press Test connection, then Save and restart. The test shows exactly which stock groups the site will receive.
    • Tally: press Check Tally and list companies, pick the distributor's company, Save and restart.
    • Status: shows the last sync, what is waiting, and a Sync now button.
The setup page opens only on this PC (http://127.0.0.1:4100) and needs the setup password. It never shows the agent secret, only whether one is set. Forgot the password? It is LOCAL_API_TOKEN in C:\ProgramData\SSVConnector\.env; open it with Notepad as an administrator.

Check it works

On the manufacturer's side, the distributor's form shows Agent 0.6.0, last contact …, Last run OK, and within the day SSV Sync Health shows it Reconciled OK within a few minutes. On the PC, the last lines of the log should read like this:

service: started ssv-agent.exe (pid 7484)
INFO  Syncing every 5 min (set in ERPNext)

What leaves the PC

Only data about the manufacturer's items leaves the PC. The agent only reads TallyPrime; it never changes the books.

  • Bill lines for the manufacturer's items: item, quantity, rate, discount, amount, godown, batch, date, voucher type, bill and reference number.
  • The party on those bills: name, GSTIN, state and price level, as kept in Tally, plus phone, address and PIN code unless the distributor keeps them on its PC. To keep them, set SHARE_RETAILER_CONTACTS=false in the agent's .env (agent 0.5.1+). The manufacturer's site then deletes any it already holds.
  • Stock of those items: opening and closing quantity and value (at the distributor's valuation), with the closing figure split by godown.
  • Run reports: agent version, sync times, and whether the last run worked.

Other brands on the same bill, bill totals, taxes, ledgers and accounts stay on the PC. Every upload is signed with the distributor's own secret and sent over HTTPS. The distributor's consent letter lists every field.

Update or remove

Automatic updates (agent 0.5 and later). When the manufacturer approves a new version, the service downloads it between syncs and checks its fingerprint (SHA-256). Then it swaps the program and restarts. If the new version fails three times within two minutes, the service puts the previous one back by itself.

  • Approve a version: the manufacturer fills three fields in SSV Settings: Approved Agent Version, Agent Download URL and Agent SHA-256.
  • Opt a distributor out: untick Update Agent Automatically on the distributor.

To update by hand, unzip the new package and run its install.cmd: settings and the store are kept. To remove, run uninstall.cmd in C:\Program Files\SSV Agent; add -Purge to delete settings and data as well.
