Distributor data-sharing consent

A letter each distributor signs before its Tally data starts flowing to the manufacturer. It says in plain words what the SSV agent sends, what it never sends, and how the distributor can stop it.

Draft for legal review

This is a starting point written from what the software actually does. It is not legal advice. Have your legal adviser review it, and fill the highlighted blanks, before any distributor signs it.

  1. Purpose.

    The Distributor allows the Manufacturer to receive, automatically from the Distributor's TallyPrime, the data listed in Annexure B. The Manufacturer may use it only to:

    • see secondary sales and stock of its products by distributor, territory, item and retailer;
    • plan supply, replenishment and schemes;
    • check that the data connection is working.
  2. Only the Manufacturer's products.

    The SSV agent on the Distributor's PC picks out the Manufacturer's items using the rule in Annexure A. Where a bill also has other manufacturers' products, only the Manufacturer's lines are sent. Other manufacturers' products, bill totals and the Distributor's accounts never leave the PC.

  3. Read only.

    The agent only reads from TallyPrime. It never enters, changes or deletes anything in the Distributor's books.

  4. How the data travels.

    The agent sends data every [5–1440] minutes while the PC and TallyPrime are running. Every upload is:

    • encrypted in transit (HTTPS);
    • signed with a secret key issued to this Distributor only.

    Where the Distributor sells for several manufacturers, uploads pass through the SSV hub. The hub delivers each manufacturer's share to that manufacturer only. It keeps no copy of the data, only a log of batch numbers and whether each was delivered.

  5. Edits and deletions.

    Changes made in Tally are sent automatically. A bill deleted in Tally is marked as deleted and left out of all reports.

  6. Use and access.

    Only the Manufacturer's authorised staff, and its IT service provider for hosting and support, may see the data.

    The Manufacturer will not disclose one distributor's data to another distributor or to any third party, except where the law requires it. Totals across distributors (for example, by region) may be used within the Manufacturer.

  7. Stock values and margins.

    Closing stock is sent with its value, as calculated by the Distributor's own stock valuation in Tally. The Manufacturer's reports show an estimated retailer margin, based on that value, only if the Distributor agrees here:

    Yes, the Manufacturer may see estimated margins.

    No, do not show margins. (default)

  8. Retailer and supplier details.

    For parties on bills that carry the Manufacturer's products, the agent sends the details kept in Tally: name, GSTIN, state and price level. For the phone number, address and PIN code, the Distributor chooses:

    Share them too.

    Keep them on the Distributor's PC. The Manufacturer then deletes any it already holds.

    The Distributor confirms that it may share what it sends for the purposes in clause 1. The Manufacturer:

    will not contact these parties using these details.

    may contact them only about [e.g. schemes and product information].

    For legal review: when a retailer is an individual or sole proprietor, its phone and address may be personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

  9. Software on the Distributor's PC.

    The agent runs as a Windows service. It may update itself to versions the Manufacturer has approved, and checks each download's fingerprint (SHA-256) before installing. Automatic updates:

    Allowed.

    Not allowed; the Distributor installs updates itself.

  10. Keeping the data.

    The Manufacturer keeps the data for [period, e.g. 3 years]. It deletes the data within [30] days after this consent ends, unless the law requires it to be kept longer.

  11. Stopping.

    Either party may end this consent with [15] days' written notice. The Distributor may also stop the flow at once by uninstalling the agent. Either way, the Manufacturer disables the Distributor's key.

  12. If something goes wrong.

    Each party tells the other promptly if it suspects that the key or the data has been exposed. The Manufacturer then issues a new key.

Before you send it: for the manufacturer

  • Decide the blanks: how long you keep data, the notice period, and whether your team may contact retailers.
  • Margins default to No. Tick Shares Cost Price on the SSV Distributor only when the distributor ticked Yes in clause 7.
  • Record the date. Keep the signed copy, and enter its date in Data Sharing Consent Date on the SSV Distributor. Do this before sending the agent secret.
  • Automatic updates: if the distributor chose "Not allowed" in clause 9, untick Update Agent Automatically on the SSV Distributor.
  • If the distributor keeps contacts on its PC (clause 8): on the agent's setup page, Options, untick Send retailers' phone, address and PIN code (agent 0.6.0 or later; older agents: SHARE_RETAILER_CONTACTS=false in the .env). On its next sync, your site deletes the phone numbers, addresses and PIN codes it holds for that distributor. The SSV Distributor then shows Retailer Contacts Withheld. Reports keep working from names and states.
  • When consent ends (clauses 10 and 11): untick Enabled on the SSV Distributor, then Actions → Delete Channel Data. That deletes everything received from that distributor and replaces its agent's secret. The record keeps a note of who deleted what, and when.
  • Keep Annexure B true. It matches agent 0.5.1. Review it whenever the agent starts sending anything new.
