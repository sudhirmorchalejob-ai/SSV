Dealer data-sharing consent

A letter each dealer signs before its Tally data starts flowing to the distributor. It says in plain words what the SSV agent sends, what it never sends, and how the dealer can stop it.

Draft for legal review

This is a starting point written from what the software actually does. It is not legal advice. Have your legal adviser review it, and fill the highlighted blanks, before any dealer signs it.

GSTIN: [GSTIN]

  1. Purpose.

    The Dealer allows the Distributor to receive, automatically from the Dealer's TallyPrime, the data listed in Annexure B. The Distributor may use it only to:

    • see secondary sales and stock of its products by dealer, territory, item and customer;
    • plan supply, replenishment and schemes;
    • check that the data connection is working.
  2. Only the Distributor's products.

    The SSV agent on the Dealer's PC picks out the Distributor's items using the rule in Annexure A. Where a bill also has other brands' products, only the Distributor's lines are sent. Other distributors' products, bill totals and the Dealer's accounts never leave the PC.

    If the Dealer also buys the same brands from another supplier, its sales of those items are sent too: Tally does not record which supplier a sold item came from.

  3. Read only.

    The agent only reads from TallyPrime. It never enters, changes or deletes anything in the Dealer's books.

  4. How the data travels.

    The agent sends data every [5–1440] minutes while the PC and TallyPrime are running. Every upload is:

    • encrypted in transit (HTTPS);
    • signed with a secret key issued to this Dealer only.

    Where the Dealer buys from several distributors, uploads pass through the SSV hub. The hub delivers each distributor's share to that distributor only. It keeps no copy of the data, only a log of batch numbers and whether each was delivered.

  5. Edits and deletions.

    Changes made in Tally are sent automatically. A bill deleted in Tally is marked as deleted and left out of all reports.

  6. Use and access.

    Only the Distributor's authorised staff, and its IT service provider for hosting and support, may see the data.

    The Distributor will not disclose one dealer's data to another dealer or to any third party, except where the law requires it. Totals across dealers (for example, by region) may be used within the Distributor.

  7. Sharing with manufacturers.

    The Distributor may pass on to the manufacturers whose products it distributes:

    nothing from this Dealer's data. (default)

    totals per item and month, without customer names or details.

    the same data it receives under this consent.

  8. Stock values and margins.

    Closing stock is sent with its value, as calculated by the Dealer's own stock valuation in Tally. The Distributor's reports show an estimated customer margin, based on that value, only if the Dealer agrees here:

    Yes, the Distributor may see estimated margins.

    No, do not show margins. (default)

  9. Customer and supplier details.

    For parties on bills that carry the Distributor's products, the agent sends the details kept in Tally: name, GSTIN, state and price level. For the phone number, address and PIN code, the Dealer chooses:

    Keep them on the Dealer's PC. (recommended: a dealer's customers are mostly individuals) The Distributor then deletes any it already holds.

    Share them too.

    The Dealer confirms that it may share what it sends for the purposes in clause 1. The Distributor:

    will not contact these parties using these details.

    may contact them only about [e.g. schemes and product information].

    For legal review: a dealer's customers are often consumers. Their names, phones and addresses are personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and cash sales usually carry no customer at all.

  10. Software on the Dealer's PC.

    The agent runs as a Windows service. It may update itself to versions the Distributor has approved, and checks each download's fingerprint (SHA-256) before installing. Automatic updates:

    Allowed.

    Not allowed; the Dealer installs updates itself.

  11. Keeping the data.

    The Distributor keeps the data for [period, e.g. 3 years]. It deletes the data within [30] days after this consent ends, unless the law requires it to be kept longer.

  12. Stopping.

    Either party may end this consent with [15] days' written notice. The Dealer may also stop the flow at once by uninstalling the agent. Either way, the Distributor disables the Dealer's key.

  13. If something goes wrong.

    Each party tells the other promptly if it suspects that the key or the data has been exposed. The Distributor then issues a new key.

Before you send it: for the distributor

  • Decide the blanks: how long you keep data, the notice period, and whether your team may contact customers.
  • Margins default to No. Tick Shares Cost Price on the SSV Dealer only when the dealer ticked Yes in clause 8.
  • Record the date. Keep the signed copy, and enter its date in Data Sharing Consent Date on the SSV Dealer. Do this before sending the agent secret.
  • Automatic updates: if the dealer chose "Not allowed" in clause 10, untick Update Agent Automatically on the SSV Dealer.
  • If the dealer keeps contacts on its PC (clause 9): on the agent's setup page, Options, untick Send retailers' phone, address and PIN code (agent 0.6.0 or later). On its next sync, your site deletes the phone numbers, addresses and PIN codes it holds for that dealer. The SSV Dealer then shows Customer Contacts Withheld. Reports keep working from names and states.
  • When consent ends (clauses 11 and 12): untick Enabled on the SSV Dealer, then Actions → Delete Channel Data. That deletes everything received from that dealer and replaces its agent's secret. The record keeps a note of who deleted what, and when.
  • Keep Annexure B true. It matches agent 0.5.1. Review it whenever the agent starts sending anything new.
